JOIN US

Opportunities

We are looking for researchers from chemical engineering, materials science, computer science, or a related field who want to build AI that understands physical systems.

OPENINGS

Positions

Postdoctoral Researcher

Ph.D. holders

We are looking for someone ready to grow into an independent researcher, gaining experience in publishing, writing proposals, and mentoring students along the way.

Graduate Student

M.S., Ph.D., or integrated M.S./Ph.D.

We are looking for students who want to become researchers who can define a problem and solve it on their own.

Undergraduate Researcher

During the semester and summer or winter breaks

We are looking for students who want to see what research is really like and figure out where they want to go next.

Who Thrives Here

  • Anyone who wants to solve chemical engineering problems with AI
  • Anyone who wants to bring their own expertise into other fields
  • Anyone who wants their research to help solve real problems in industry

What You Will Learn

  • Designing your own models that combine physics with AI
  • Building trustworthy predictive models from small datasets
  • Finding the reasons behind a model's predictions

NEXT STEPS

How to Apply

  1. 01

    Contact

    Email us a short introduction and what you are interested in. You are welcome to reach out even if you are still deciding on graduate school. No prior experience in coding or AI is required.

  2. 02

    Meeting

    We will talk about the lab's work and your interests. It is not an interview, just a chance to get to know each other. Ask anything: daily life in the lab, funding, careers after graduation.

  3. 03

    Decision

    After the meeting, you and we each consider whether we are a good match. No hurry, and no pressure. We decide together once we are both sure.

  4. 04

    Joining

    Welcome to HANA Lab. We pick a research topic together and make the plans you need. Regular meetings keep us both on top of progress and problems.

Questions? Email us anytime. We look forward to working with you at HANA Lab.
